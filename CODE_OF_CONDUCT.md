# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Windows Live to be welcoming, respectful, and safe for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code; do not attack people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on what helps the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information, private messages, or unredacted logs without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant and follow the repository's contribution and security-reporting guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer using the contact route on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. The maintainer will review reports and may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to protect the community.

This policy applies to everyone participating in project spaces and is enforced by the project maintainer.
